Privacy & Terms

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

What we collect: when you sign in with Google, we receive your display name, email address, and profile picture (if any). We also store what you enter directly in the app: recipes, ingredients, notes, photos you upload, categories, and tags.

What we don't collect: we never request or access your Google Drive, Contacts, Calendar, or anything beyond the above.

How we use your data: to display and sync your recipes across your devices. We never sell or rent your data, and we don't use it for advertising.

Where it's stored: on Firebase (Google Cloud), which acts as our data processor under its own security measures.

Imported recipes: if you import a recipe from a link (YouTube, TikTok, etc.), we only save the link, title, category, and your own note — never the creator's full content. The source stays visible and credited.

Shared recipes: if you choose "Share to Get Inspired" on a recipe, that recipe becomes visible to all signed-in users of the app. Your name and profile picture are not shown with it. You can stop sharing anytime, and the recipe will no longer be visible to others.

Your rights: you can download a copy of all your data using "Export my data", or permanently delete your account and all your data using "Delete account" — both available from the menu under your profile picture. You can also revoke the app's access from your Google account settings.

Minimum age: this app is intended for users 18 and older.
